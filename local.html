Welcome to The Daily Grind!

We craft high-quality, ethically-sourced coffee beans in a cozy, welcoming environment. Whether you need a quick morning pick-me-up or a relaxing spot to catch up with friends, we have a seat waiting for you.

Our Story & Passion

Founded in 2018, The Daily Grind started as a small coffee cart on the corner of 5th Avenue. Today, we are proud to be a staple of the local community, serving hundreds of smiling faces every single day.

Our Commitment

We work directly with sustainable family farms across Latin America and East Africa. This guarantees that our coffee farmers are paid fairly and you receive the highest grade beans possible.
